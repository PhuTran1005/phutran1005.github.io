A guest lecture for KAIST CS632 (Embedded Operating Systems), Fall 2026, on image and video super-resolution from a model-centric perspective.

The lecture covers super-resolution fundamentals and evaluation, non-generative and generative models, video super-resolution, and practical trade-offs among fidelity, realism, and efficiency. The presentation contains 62 slides.
